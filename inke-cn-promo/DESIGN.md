# Design System

## Overview

Inke's captured homepage uses a clean white canvas with black navigation, compact Chinese typography, and rounded media cards. The visual identity is friendly and consumer-facing: a teal app mark, playful floating gradients, soft rings, and a crown illustration sit around a large live-room module. The page mixes live-stream discovery with editorial "直播故事" content and direct mobile-download cues through QR codes. The overall feel is bright, social, lightweight, and app-centric.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#FFFFFF` — dominant page background and card surface.
- **Primary Text**: `#000000` — navigation and strong headings.
- **Body Text**: `#333333` — article titles and readable interface text.
- **Muted Text**: `#999999` — metadata, secondary labels, footer text.
- **Soft Divider**: `#D8D8D8` — subtle separators and low-emphasis structure.
- **Accent Teal**: `#00D8C9` — brand icon background, active states, buttons, outlines.
- **Accent Deep Teal**: `#129289` — darker teal support tone.
- **Accent Warm**: `#FFA154` — small live-room badges and warm highlights.

## Typography

- **Primary Chinese Sans**: PingFangSC-Medium (500). Used for navigation, headings, and card labels.
- **Regular Chinese Sans**: PingFangSC-Regular (400). Used for supporting copy, descriptions, and footer information.
- **Hierarchy**: Compact UI labels around 16-20px, section headers around 26-34px, promo titles can scale up to 88-124px while preserving PingFang SC shapes.

## Elevation

Depth is soft and practical rather than glossy. Cards use rounded corners, clipped imagery, light borders, and subtle shadow-like contrast from image overlays. The hero area creates depth through a large video rectangle, small stacked preview cards, and decorative teal rings floating behind the content.

## Components

- **Top Navigation Bar**: White header with app icon, black Chinese menu labels, and pill actions outlined or filled in `#00D8C9`.
- **Live Room Stage**: Large rounded media viewport with a dark translucent streamer profile badge, QR download chip, and right-side stacked live thumbnails.
- **Floating Brand Decorations**: Teal-blue gradients, rings, small dots, and crown artwork around the hero.
- **Story Cards**: Rounded image cards with dark bottom gradients and Chinese article captions.
- **Live Grid**: Four-column image grid with streamer thumbnails and compact name labels.
- **Download QR Chip**: Small rounded rectangle pairing "手机客户端下载安装" with QR code.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use `#FFFFFF` as the dominant background and reserve `#00D8C9` for brand emphasis.
- Keep Chinese text compact, clear, and high-contrast using PingFang SC.
- Use rounded media cards, clipped imagery, and dark caption gradients over photos.
- Let teal rings, dots, and soft glow shapes add motion and friendliness.
- Show the app icon or brand mark at the beginning and end.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the promo into a dark tech reel; the captured page is bright and consumer-facing.
- Do not invent statistics or product claims that are not present in the capture.
- Do not use heavy glassmorphism or neon purple-blue gradients.
- Do not rely on text-only beats; use the app icon, QR, story photos, and media cards.
- Do not make the QR code or footer/legal copy the primary visual focus.
